About Maxlong

Twenty years of making panel components. Now with a home in the U.S.

Maxlong Inc. connects U.S. panel builders directly with our parent manufacturer in Yueqing, Wenzhou — one of China’s major centers for electrical components.

Our story

From our factory floor to your panel.

For 20 years our parent company has designed and manufactured panel accessories in-house: research and development, tooling and production under one roof.

In October 2026 we founded Maxlong Inc. in Sacramento, California, so U.S. customers can buy from the factory with a local point of contact — for quotes, samples, orders and support.
